# Read it, run it, fork it — and we will never chase you.

That sentence above is the deal: the free layer of this repo is yours, forever,
no strings. What follows is what is *deliberately not* in the repo, why, and
how to ask for it if you actually need it.

## What is behind the lock (and why it is not here)

| Locked item | Why the free layer is not enough without it | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Production calibration recipes (NMR cost anchor derivation, threshold calibration methodology, hash-chain production parameters) | The free checker tells you *whether* you pass; the recipes make your numbers *trustworthy at enterprise grade*. Without them, your self-report is still self-report. | The exact derivation, boundary-sample construction method, and production parameter sets — plus a working session to calibrate **your** system |
| Industry recipe template library (support / e-commerce / brand-voice prompt recipes) | Generic clauses are a bar to clear; these are field-tested shortcuts for your vertical | Vertical-specific templates + adaptation notes |
| Enterprise self-assessment & certification service | Self-proving L3 is possible alone; a third-party conformance report is what your buyers accept | Audit-grade report with evidence chain |
| Custom adaptation (embedding/A2A/production wiring) | Your stack is not our toy example | Integration work against your real system |

All locked items are **explicitly not in this repository**. Nothing here is a
teaser for hidden files — the repo is complete at what it claims to be.

## How to ask (two things, no more)

1. What are you building?
2. Which locked item do you need + what have you already tried?

One-line messages get one-line replies.

## Not a fit / A fit

**Not a fit:** you want the recipes for free "to evaluate" (the free layer *is*
the evaluation); you want us to implement your product; you want exclusivity on
a public standard.

**A fit:** you run a memory layer or an agent platform and need an independent
conformance story for enterprise buyers; you want to co-build the benchmark and
get cited as a co-author; you found a failure mode we have not covered — open an
issue describing it, the sharpest ones go into the next version with your name on it.
